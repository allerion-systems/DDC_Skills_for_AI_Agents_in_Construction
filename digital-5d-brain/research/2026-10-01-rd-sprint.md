## 2. Roofing material that *is* a solar panel — "SeamBus Roof"

Prior art exists (Tesla Solar Roof, GAF Timberline Solar, CertainTeed Apollo, Luma, SunTegra, standing-seam BIPV vendors) — differentiation must come from installation labor, cost, and repairability, not from the idea itself.

**Concept:** roll-formed standing-seam steel/aluminum roof panel with factory-laminated thin-film PV (flexible CIGS now; perovskite/Si tandem when bankable) under an ETFE or fluoropolymer glazing film.

Key inventions to explore:
1. **Seam-as-bus:** the mechanical standing seam also carries the electrical connection (insulated conductive strip in the seam gasket). Closing the seam = making the circuit. No separate wiring step → roofer labor only.
2. **Panel = weather barrier first:** passes roofing standards (wind uplift, fire class, impact) independent of PV function; if PV fails, the roof still works.
3. **Cold-side heat harvest:** vented air gap beneath the panel feeds a heat-recovery plenum (DHW preheat / heat-pump source), cooling cells and raising net yield.
4. **Field-replaceable cells:** snap-in PV strips on a roof-grade carrier so damaged areas replace like shingles.
5. **Fault isolation:** module-level power electronics (MLPE) or optimizer-in-seam-clip for rapid shutdown compliance.

Trade-offs: lower efficiency than rack-mounted c-Si; heat derating on dark metal; fluoropolymer film adds cost and PV-grade fluorine supply ties back to fluorspar (see §4).

**Verify next:** UL 7103 / UL 61730 / ASTM E1592 / Class A fire path; $/W installed vs. rack PV + metal roof; perovskite stability data.

## 3. New way of building batteries — "Brick, not pack"

Design thesis: cheap, safe, serviceable stationary storage for edge sites; mobility is secondary.

1. **Chemistry:** sodium-ion (hard carbon anode, layered-oxide or Prussian-white cathode) or LFP. No cobalt, low/no lithium dependence (Na-ion), tolerates full discharge to 0 V shipping.
2. **Fluorine-light cell:** water-processed electrodes with CMC/SBR binder instead of PVDF/NMP; explore low-fluorine electrolyte salts. Cuts a fluorspar-chain dependency and toxic solvent handling.
3. **Dry-electrode + laser-structured thick electrodes:** solvent-free film → fewer drying ovens (big capex/energy cut); laser-drilled channels for fast ion transport at thick loadings.
4. **Cell-to-structure "brick":** 5–10 kWh sealed module with integrated cold plate, busbars and BMS board; bricks hot-swap like server blades (1 person, no tools beyond a driver).
5. **Fault containment by design:** intumescent inter-brick barriers + per-brick vent channel; BMS reports impedance spectroscopy per brick for early degradation flags.
6. **Second-life ready:** standard brick dimensions across generations so old bricks re-pool into low-duty applications.

**Verify next:** current Na-ion cycle life/energy density from commercial datasheets; UL 9540/9540A and NFPA 855 pathway; dry-electrode patent landscape.

## 4. Fluorspar (CaF2): uses and fusion relevance

### 4.1 Major uses
- **Acid-grade fluorspar (>97% CaF2) → hydrofluoric acid (HF):** gateway to nearly all fluorochemicals.
  - Refrigerants/propellants (HFCs, HFOs), fluoropolymers (PTFE, PVDF, FEP, ETFE, PVF), pharmaceuticals/agrochemicals.
  - **Energy:** LiPF6 electrolyte, PVDF binder (Li-ion), fluoropolymer PV backsheets/glazing films, UF6 for enrichment, NF3/SF6 semiconductor and electrical uses.
  - Aluminum smelting (AlF3/cryolite).
- **Metallurgical grade (60–85%):** flux in steelmaking, ferroalloys.
- **Ceramic/glass/cement/optics:** opacifiers, enamel, glass, CaF2 UV/IR optics, lenses and windows.
- **Supply-chain context:** fluorspar is on the USGS critical minerals list; the Illinois–Kentucky Fluorspar District (Hardin/Pope Cos., IL) is the historic U.S. source. *Per the Digital Twin rules, Hicks Dome is adjacent, not on-property.*

### 4.2 Fusion relevance
| Use | How | Notes / caveats |
|---|---|---|
| **FLiBe breeder-coolant** (2LiF·BeF2) | Fluorspar → HF → LiF and BeF2. Molten salt blanket breeds tritium (Li), multiplies neutrons (Be), and carries heat (low pressure, high-temp). | Needs Li-6 enrichment; Be toxicity; tritium extraction from salt is hard (TF/T2 speciation); corrosion control via redox (e.g., Be metal); melting point ~460 °C limits cold-leg design. Proposed in concepts such as ARC-class tokamaks and FFHR-type designs. |
| **FLiNaK / alt fluoride salts** | Lower-cost, Be-free coolant loops, intermediate heat transfer. | No neutron multiplier; weaker breeding. |
| **Tritium-handling chemistry** | Fluorination/HF processes for isotope/salt cleanup; fluorinated getters study. | Highly specialized. |
| **Plasma/semiconductor + diagnostics** | NF3/SF6 etch for magnet/electronics fabrication; SF6 in HV insulation; CaF2 windows. | Radiation darkening limits optics in-vessel. |
| **Fluoropolymers** | Cable insulation, seals, cryostat parts. | **PTFE degrades badly under radiation** — place only in low-dose zones. |

**Insight:** fusion's fluorine demand is small vs. batteries/refrigerants, but FLiBe is a strategic *pull* for high-purity LiF/BeF2 and for isotopically enriched Li-6 supply chains. Rare-earth/fluorspar co-location at Hicks-Dome-type systems is a potential domestic-supply narrative — **only if** purity, beryllium handling, and Li-6 supply are solved.

**Verify next:** FLiBe tritium solubility/extraction data (ARC, MIT, UKAEA/Kyoto papers); US fluorspar production/import numbers (USGS MCS 2026); HF-grade purity specs for LiF/BeF2 breeder salt.
